# 0085 — Queue-drain entry mode on /ship

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-02

> **Number re-derived at landing (the #1290 convention).** `decisions/` ends at **0083** (`0083-honest-substrate.md`, landed with PRD #1266 slice #1310). **0082** is reserved for the standing-promotion-ack draft, adr-critic-approved and parked on issue #1252 — the reservation is recorded as a note in `README.md`. **0084** is provisionally claimed by the public-repo portability draft, round-3 strict-stopped on issue #1325. **0085** is therefore the next genuinely-free integer at landing time, which is also the integer the draft carried; no propagation was required.

**Extends:** [ADR-0081](0081-post-audit-dead-weight-retirements.md) D4 (`/ship` is the single lifecycle orchestrator — the drain is an entry mode on it, not a sibling), [ADR-0008](0008-workflow-autolog-bootstrap-and-naming.md) D1/D2 (the captured→backlog autopilot — consumed unchanged as the sole gate through which captured items enter execution) and [ADR-0008](0008-workflow-autolog-bootstrap-and-naming.md) D3 (whose closing clause explicitly anticipates a later sweep of captures that were created outside an active agent context — affirmative license for what the drain does to the standing captured tier), [ADR-0010](0010-implementer-subagent-auto-pipeline.md) D3 (DAG-aware parallel execution — lifted from slice level to queue level), [ADR-0062](0062-merge-integrity-green-main.md) D2 (merges serialize; implementation stays parallel — unchanged at queue scale), [ADR-0040](0040-qa-human-residual-model.md) D2 (the async, non-blocking operator queue and its `needs-human-check` label — reused as the drain's triage-escalation surface), [ADR-0006](0006-backlog-and-session-continuity.md) D2 (live-state reconstruction — the run ledger supplements it for in-run state without becoming a formal cross-session handoff document), [ADR-0075](0075-trace-core-fork-decisions.md) D2 (append-only JSONL at the git-common-dir root — the ledger adopts the same path resolution), [ADR-0056](0056-no-rule-without-a-check.md) D1 (rule #23 — every new obligation below ships with its check or an explicit advisory tag).

**Bootstrap-mode ([ADR-0004](0004-bypass-prevention.md) D2):** binds forward from the implementing PRD's slices. Existing open issues are consumed by the drain as they stand — no retroactive re-shaping of issue bodies, and past sessions' improvised whole-queue practice is history, not violation.

## Context

The project carries a standing operator goal — fix or close every open issue before tagging v1.0.0 — against a queue that, **at drafting time (2026-09-02)**, held 172 open items (1 `prd`, 4 `slice`, 67 `backlog`, 100 `captured`, plus 1 open non-draft PR). Every figure in this ADR is a drafting-time snapshot and is expected to drift; nothing downstream depends on it, because the mode re-counts the queue live at every run start and records that count in the run's own `run_start` record. `/ship`'s Conduct section already claims run-to-done semantics including "ship everything in the backlog", but nothing defines how a whole-queue run works: queue assembly, the per-item autonomy boundary, survival across a quota cut, parallelism without conflicts, or what happens to small fixes discovered mid-run (today they become yet more captured issues — the operator explicitly called this out as bad practice). A full-queue triage found only ~2 genuine design forks plus one bulk-close ack among all open items: the queue is overwhelmingly autonomously drainable, and the missing piece is orchestration contract, not capability. A grill session on 2026-09-02 settled six design forks (recorded, together with the triage-cluster table grounding the drainability claim, in `docs/decision-log/2026-09-02-queue-drain-mode.md`); this ADR encodes the ones that constrain future work.

## Decisions

### D1 — Queue-drain is an entry mode on `/ship`, never a second orchestrator

`/ship` gains a **Queue-drain entry mode** with a normative trigger set: "drain the queue", "/ship drain", "deploy all open PRDs". On a trigger, the orchestrator assembles the run queue from all open `prd`-, `slice`-, `backlog`-, and `captured`-labeled issues plus open non-draft PRs, and drives it run-to-done through the existing stages and guarded verbs. Two bounded sub-forms exist: a **plan-only form** ("drain plan") performs queue assembly + triage + lane construction and writes the ledger with zero dispatches and zero GitHub mutations — the cheap, real-data inspection surface; and a **bounded form** ("drain N item(s)") runs the full lifecycle limited to the first N queue items in lane order — the smoke-test and walking-skeleton vehicle.

**Captured items enter execution only through the existing captured→backlog autopilot ([ADR-0008](0008-workflow-autolog-bootstrap-and-naming.md) D2, CAP-003):** the drain invokes `backlog-critic` per captured item; APPROVE promotes it to `backlog` and it proceeds as backlog work; BLOCK leaves it in the captured tier for the operator's lazy review — the drain never implements a BLOCKed captured item, and may at most bundle BLOCKed items into one class-ack escalation (D2). ADR-0008's disposition is consumed, not overridden. That the drain reaches captures which were never auto-processed at creation time is not an extension of ADR-0008 but the discharge of its own [D3](0008-workflow-autolog-bootstrap-and-naming.md) closing clause, which parks exactly those captures until "a future session-start sweep" arrives. Backlog-tier items ride the standard PRD pipeline, with slices minted only through the mandatory slicer flow (rule #16; CI CHECK 19 `Slicer-provenance:` trailer).

No new skill, subagent, or critic is created (ADR-0046 D1 parsimony; ADR-0081 D4 made `/ship` sole orchestrator precisely because the last second-conductor produced drift).

### D2 — The autonomy boundary is the reasonable-engineer litmus; escalation is label-and-continue on a two-label split

Triage classifies each queue item into three buckets using the grill litmus — *would a reasonable senior engineer need to ask the owner before doing this?*:

- **Autonomous** (the default): proceeds through the pipeline unattended.
- **Operator-owed** (genuine design forks, destructive/irreversible calls): gets the **`needs-human-check`** label — [ADR-0040](0040-qa-human-residual-model.md) D2's non-blocking residual surface, deliberately NOT the I5 `needs-human` label, because [ADR-0070](0070-two-tier-autonomous-delivery.md) D2 condition (e) makes RELEASE-READY require zero open `needs-human` items and a whole-queue drain that parked its design forks on that label would freeze `main` promotion for the entire run and until the operator cleared every fork — plus an entry on the operator's async queue, and **the run continues past it**: never stop-and-ask, so the mode holds with or without a live operator.
- **Class-ack** (bulk closures of a coherent class, e.g. obsolete-by-supersession captures): ONE class-level escalation covering the whole class on the same `needs-human-check` surface, never per-item asks; the drain never closes a class unilaterally.

Because `needs-human-check` now carries two distinct meanings — QA residuals ([ADR-0040](0040-qa-human-residual-model.md) D1) and drain triage escalations — drain-authored escalations are titled with a **`Drain triage:` prefix** so the operator's single label query stays readable. The prefix is a naming convention, not a gate `(advisory — no check reads the prefix; its evidence trigger is an operator complaint that the residual queue has become unsortable)`.

Every classification is **recorded**: each queue item gets a `triaged` ledger record naming its bucket and carrying its lane assignment as a field (no separate lane record kind exists — the kind set in D4 is closed), and every escalation appends an `escalated` record that carries its applied-label evidence at write time (`label` + `label_applied: true`) — the durable fact the DRAIN-LEDGER health row (D6) asserts, decidable from the ledger alone. The row also FAILs any `item_start` lacking a prior `triaged` record — the recording obligation is deterministic. The classification's *correctness* is judgment and stays with the orchestrator `(advisory — evidence triggers: an operator reversal of a drained item's outcome, or a root-cause capture naming a triage misclassification)`.

Hard invariants are absolute and unaffected by any triage verdict: the drain never creates `.claude/PROMOTE_OK` ([ADR-0070](0070-two-tier-autonomous-delivery.md) **D4** — the meta-tripwire decision that owns the sentinel; note that CLAUDE.md §1 rule #4 miscites D1 for this mechanism, recorded on issue #1252, and this ADR deliberately does not propagate that miscite); a round-3 critic BLOCK strict-stops that item via the I5 surface — there and only there the drain applies the `needs-human` label (ADR-0048 D1), accepting that a genuinely stuck item holds the ADR-0070 D2 promotion gate, which is that gate working as intended — while unrelated lanes continue (per-item containment mirroring ADR-0010 D4 forward-block); destructive operations confirm first; worktree isolation per ADR-0036 D1 with the asserted-interface contract of [ADR-0058](0058-worktree-isolation-as-asserted-interface.md) D1/D2 (missing `worktreePath` = dispatch failure; agents self-assert isolation); slice issues are created only through the mandatory slicer flow (rule #16).

### D3 — Parallelism is the slice model lifted to queue level

Triage builds a coarse **file-overlap DAG** over queue items (predicted from paths named in issue bodies/titles): overlapping items serialize into a lane; independent lanes run in parallel isolated worktrees under a **concurrency cap of 3**. The cap's value is the operator's initial setting from the 2026-09-02 grill (Q5 — "modest cap ~3", recorded in the decision-log entry), motivated by GitHub secondary-rate-limit evidence (#1073) and review-quality concerns; it is deterministically enforced — the DRAIN-LEDGER row (D6) FAILs a ledger showing more than 3 concurrently-open `item_start` records — and revisited on evidence `(advisory revisit trigger: lanes sitting idle against a deep queue, or review-quality degradation at the cap)`. Unknown overlap defaults to serialize `(advisory — the default is triage judgment; its evidence trigger is a merge conflict between items triaged as independent)`. Merges stay strictly serialized through the PR gate (ADR-0062 D2). Overlap prediction is deliberately a heuristic, not a static analyzer — a wrong guess costs a merge conflict caught at the PR gate. GitHub mutations are paced across the run `(advisory — no quantified cooldown exists yet; a drain run hitting secondary-rate-limit errors is the evidence trigger to mechanize pacing)`.

### D4 — A durable run ledger, separate from trace-v3

Each drain run appends to `.claude/logs/drain/<run-id>.jsonl`, resolved against the **git-common-dir root** (the [ADR-0075](0075-trace-core-fork-decisions.md) D2 convention, so worktree sessions share one ledger), with a **closed kind set**: `run_start` (queue snapshot: per-label open-issue counts + open non-draft PR count), `triaged`, `item_start`, `item_done`, `escalated`, `fix_queued`, `fixed_in_run`, `parked`, `resumed`, `run_end`. Lane assignment is a **field on `triaged`**, not a kind of its own; the set above admits no additions without a superseding ADR.

The ledger is the run's durable state across session death and quota parks: a `parked` record names the remaining items in resume order (an empty remaining-items list is a schema FAIL in D6), and that list **must include every `fix_queued` item still unlanded at park time** — otherwise a run that parks and never reaches `run_end` could lose a queued fix through the gap between the two terminals (D6's parity assertion therefore closes at `run_end` **or** `parked`). A resumed session reads the ledger rather than reconstructing intent from memory. The captured-issue reference that discharges an unlanded `fix_queued` is carried in a pinned field, **`captured_ref`**, on the `fix_queued` record, so the health row's field list is fully determined rather than pattern-matched.

The ledger is deliberately **not** new trace-v3 kinds — `tools/trace.py`'s closed enum (PIP-015) and the Run-board's spans-only contract (PIP-022) are untouched; dispatch/PR truth stays in trace-v3, and the ledger records only drain-run orchestration state. Because trace-v3 remains the dispatch truth, it also serves as the plan-only form's **independent witness**: a plan-only run appends zero `dispatch` spans there (validated against a live positive control — the bounded real drain must demonstrably increment the span count in the same environment, else the zero-witness is vacuous and the production check FAILs). The park trigger itself (quota proximity) stays orchestrator judgment `(advisory — no quota-remaining API exists; the checkable obligation is the parked record, not the detection heuristic)`.

### D5 — Fix-in-run: trivial-lane discoveries land inside the drain run

A mid-run discovery whose remedy fits the trivial lane (I3 — definition unchanged) is **appended to the current drain run's queue** as a `fix_queued` ledger record and lands as its own `hotfix/*` PR before the drain run ends — recorded as `fixed_in_run` referencing the merged PR — instead of being filed as a captured issue and deferred. If the drain run ends with the item unlanded, it MUST be captured then, and the ledger must reference the captured issue in the `fix_queued` record's `captured_ref` field before the run's terminal record — enforced deterministically by the DRAIN-LEDGER **fix-queued parity** assertion (D6): every `fix_queued` without a matching `fixed_in_run` must carry a `captured_ref` before `run_end`, or be named in the `parked` record's remaining-items list, else FAIL. No silent loss on either terminal; CAP-001 is honored because only genuinely deferred items require capture. Boundaries unchanged: discoveries too big for the trivial lane capture as before; workflow mistakes still produce root-cause captures per rule #13 (fix-in-run covers the code remedy, never the capture obligation); code-defect fixes keep the regression rider (ADR-0067 D2/D3). Plain single-feature `/ship` runs are untouched — fix-in-run is a drain-run protocol.

### D6 — Enforcement (rule #23) and the generated rules

- **CI (deterministic):** `tools/ci-checks.sh` gains a check that (a) delegates to the health registry in the CHECK 22/23 form — `python3 dashboard/health.py --check DRAIN-LEDGER`, passing on exit 0 and failing on exit 1 — so the criterion binds to the row's behaviour rather than to the check's own banner text, and (b) asserts the grill-me handoff no longer references the aspirational "/goal" placeholder (count = 0).
- **Health row (deterministic, offline-decidable):** `DRAIN-LEDGER` in `dashboard/health.py` validates the newest ledger **from the ledger file alone — it never queries GitHub**. Every FAIL condition was re-derived against the test *"is this evaluable from the ledger, or from state that correct operator behaviour can mutate?"* after round-2 review showed a live-parity predicate (escalated ⇒ currently-open `needs-human` artifact) inverting on the operator's success path — answering an escalation would have flipped a correct historical ledger to FAIL. That is exactly the failure [ADR-0083](0083-honest-substrate.md) D3 names: a check may only assert an invariant its subjects are contractually obliged to satisfy, and current GitHub label state is not an invariant a historical ledger owes. FAIL on any of: malformed JSONL or missing required record fields (`run_start` count fields included); a kind outside the closed set; an `escalated` record missing its recorded applied-label evidence (`label` ∈ {`needs-human-check`, `needs-human`} + `label_applied: true`, written at escalation time); an `item_start` without a prior `triaged` record; more than 3 concurrently-open `item_start` records; a `fix_queued` unresolved at the run's terminal record (no `fixed_in_run`, no `captured_ref`, and — when the terminal is `parked` — not named in its remaining-items list); a `parked` record with an empty remaining-items list. No ledger yet → WARN, not FAIL. Because the row makes no `gh` calls, it has no network-degradation mode (contrast `check_record_vs_gh`'s "unverifiable" path). The ledger path is **injectable** so tests exercise fixtures without writing into `.claude/logs/` (rule #21 / CRI-004); the default resolution is the git-common-dir pattern. CLI output follows the registry's prose contract — `PASS: DRAIN-LEDGER — <detail>`, exit 0 on PASS/WARN, 1 on FAIL (no JSON mode exists; #1324). A pytest regression covers the PASS leg and at least the unknown-kind and fix-queued-parity FAIL legs.
- **Advisory (tagged inline, each with its evidence trigger):** triage-classification correctness (D2), the `Drain triage:` title prefix (D2), the cap's *value* and the unknown-overlap serialize default (D3), mutation pacing (D3), and the park-detection heuristic (D4).

**Decision → rule mapping** (rendered by `tools/gen_rules.py`; the four texts below are added to its rule-statement table and `RULE_IDS_BASELINE` is bumped by 4):

- **PIP-026** (from D1/D2): *`/ship`'s queue-drain entry mode is the sole whole-queue drain path: the queue assembles from open `prd`/`slice`/`backlog`/`captured` issues plus open non-draft PRs; captured items enter execution only via the captured→backlog autopilot (ADR-0008 D2, the sweep ADR-0008 D3 anticipates) and backlog items ride the standard PRD→slicer flow (rule #16); triage uses the reasonable-engineer litmus with label-and-continue escalation onto `needs-human-check` — never `needs-human`, which stays the round-3/I5 strict-stop surface that gates ADR-0070 D2 promotion — and the run never blocks on a human answer; hard invariants (no agent-created PROMOTE_OK per ADR-0070 D4, round-3 strict-stop, destructive-op confirmation, worktree isolation, slicer-only slice creation) are absolute and not triage-overridable (ADR-0085 D1/D2).*
- **PIP-027** (from D3): *Drain parallelism lifts the slice model to queue level: file-overlap lanes, independent lanes parallel in isolated worktrees, at most 3 items concurrently in flight (enforced by DRAIN-LEDGER), unknown overlap serializes, merges serialize through the PR gate per ADR-0062 D2 (ADR-0085 D3).*
- **PIP-028** (from D4/D6): *Every drain run appends to `.claude/logs/drain/<run-id>.jsonl` at the git-common-dir root with the closed kind set {run_start, triaged, item_start, item_done, escalated, fix_queued, fixed_in_run, parked, resumed, run_end} and lane assignment as a field on `triaged`; trace-v3's enum is untouched; a park writes a `parked` record naming remaining items in resume order, including every still-unlanded `fix_queued`; the DRAIN-LEDGER health row enforces schema, kinds, recorded-escalation completeness, triage-precedence, the concurrency bound, and fix-queued parity — evaluated from the ledger alone, never from live GitHub state (ADR-0085 D4/D6).*
- **PIP-029** (from D5): *Trivial-lane discoveries land inside the drain run as their own `hotfix/*` PRs recorded `fixed_in_run`; a drain run may not reach its terminal record with a `fix_queued` item that lacks a `fixed_in_run`, a `captured_ref`, or (on a park) a place in the remaining-items list; I3's definition, rule #13 root-cause captures, and the ADR-0067 regression rider are unchanged (ADR-0085 D5).*

## Consequences

- **Triage misclassification is possible**: an item the operator would have wanted to see may drain autonomously. Mitigations: the hard invariants are not triage-overridable, every change still passes the reviewer gate, every classification is recorded in the ledger, and every action lands in the GitHub audit trail. The residual risk is accepted as the price of runs that survive operator absence.
- **Two escalation labels now coexist by design**: `needs-human-check` (triage-time, non-blocking — promotion stays available mid-drain) and `needs-human` (round-3 strict-stop — holds the ADR-0070 D2 promotion gate, intentionally). The cost is that "what's waiting on me" spans two label queries; the async operator queue already aggregates both, and the `Drain triage:` title prefix keeps the merged queue sortable.
- **The run ledger does not reopen [ADR-0006](0006-backlog-and-session-continuity.md) D2's rejection of a handoff document.** D2 rejected an agent-authored narrative artifact that a later session would have to trust in place of live state; the drain ledger is neither narrative nor authoritative — it is an append-only machine record of what this run did, and the trace-v3 ledger established exactly this shape under [ADR-0075](0075-trace-core-fork-decisions.md) D2 without disturbing D2's disposition. Live state (issues, labels, PRs, git log) remains the source of truth on resume; the ledger only spares the resumed session from re-deriving intent it already recorded.
- **The ledger is machine-local** (`.claude/logs/` is gitignored): cross-machine resume is impossible. Accepted — single-operator project; the GitHub side (labels, PRs, issues) remains the shared truth per CAP-002.
- **`/ship`'s prose grows** by one substantial section — the cost of parsimony (Q1) over a second skill file.
- **Fix-in-run raises in-run mutation volume**, which is why pacing is named (advisory) in D3 rather than ignored.
- **BLOCKed captured items are not drainable** — the autopilot gate (D1) means a drain run can finish with the captured graveyard intact; clearing it stays operator work (per-item rescue/cull, or a class-ack the operator answers). This is deliberate: ADR-0008's human-review disposition survives the drain.
- **Later drain waves dogfood the mode** on the live v1 queue — defects in the mode surface as root-cause captures on real runs, which is the intended feedback loop.

## Alternatives considered

- **A dedicated `/drain` (or `/goal`) orchestrator skill** — rejected: recreates the exact two-orchestrator drift ADR-0081 D4 retired `/build` to end; every step it would run already lives in `/ship`.
- **Triage-time escalation onto the I5 `needs-human` label** — rejected: ADR-0070 D2 condition (e) counts open `needs-human` items into RELEASE-READY, so parking ordinary design forks there would deterministically freeze `main` promotion for the whole drain and until the operator cleared every fork — inverting the ADR's own motivation (ship the v1 queue). Every precedent use of `needs-human` (ADR-0004 D1, ADR-0010 D4, ADR-0048 D1) is a stop-something escalation; the non-blocking precedent surface is ADR-0040 D2's `needs-human-check`, which the drain adopts.
- **Live-parity ledger enforcement** (escalated record ⇒ currently-open labeled artifact, RECORD-VS-GH-style) — rejected: the predicate measures current operator-queue depth, not recording discipline, and FAILs on the operator's correct behavior (answering the escalation) — an invariant the ledger does not owe, per ADR-0083 D3. Write-time recorded evidence keeps the row honest and offline-decidable.
- **Keying fix-queued parity on `run_end` alone** — rejected during landing: a run that parks and dies never writes `run_end`, so an unlanded `fix_queued` would escape the parity assertion through the park path. The parity window closes at either terminal, and `parked` discharges an unlanded fix only by naming it in the resume list.
- **Headless scheduled drain runs** (`claude -p` cron) — deferred, tracked as issue #1320 with explicit revisit criteria: adds auth machinery and removes the live overseer while nothing in the current queue needs it. The mode's operator-independence (D2) deliberately keeps this door open.
- **Extending trace-v3 with drain kinds** — rejected: PIP-015's closed enum and the Run-board's recorded-spans-only contract (PIP-022) exist to keep the span ledger small and trustworthy; drain orchestration state is a different lifecycle with different consumers.
- **Stop-and-ask escalation** — rejected: kills run-to-done and reintroduces the blocking human gate ADR-0003 D4 removed; the async label-and-continue surface (ADR-0040 D2) already exists and is phone-answerable.
- **Unbounded parallelism** — rejected: GitHub secondary rate limits (#1073) and review-quality degradation argue for a modest bound; the specific value 3 is the operator's initial setting from the grill, deterministically enforced and revisited on evidence, not an observed optimum.
- **Bypassing the captured→backlog autopilot for drain runs** — rejected: the great majority of the queue sits in the captured tier precisely because ADR-0008 D2 routes their promotion through `backlog-critic` with BLOCK meaning human review; letting the drain work them directly would silently repeal that disposition on most of the queue.
- **A sequencing-as-code executor for the drain loop** — rejected here and re-affirmed as deferred (ADR-0076 D6): its recorded evidence trigger has not fired; sequencing stays LLM judgment over guarded verbs.
